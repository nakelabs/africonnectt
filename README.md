# AfriConnect

AfriConnect was built for the StacStart Hackathon.

## The Problem Statement

Cross-border fundraising for African startups is currently broken and filled with unnecessary friction:

* For Founders: The process often involves spending months sending cold emails into the void. Access to global capital networks is heavily gated, and valuable time is wasted pitching to misaligned investors who do not understand the local market.
* For Investors: Global investors face a remarkably high noise-to-signal ratio. It is incredibly difficult to discover quality deal flow, verify traction in emerging markets, and conduct fragmented due diligence from thousands of miles away.

## Our Solution

AfriConnect is a dedicated, two-sided ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between high-potential African startups and global investors. We streamline the deal-flow process, replacing cold emails and fragmented networking with a unified platform for discovering, evaluating, and closing investment opportunities. 

What our solution provides:

* Trust and Verification: Trust is the single biggest barrier to cross-border investment. Every founder and investor on AfriConnect goes through a rigorous manual verification process, including identity verification, business entity audits, and investor mandate checks before they can access the platform.
* Precision Matching: Utilizing advanced filtering by sector, growth stage, ticket size, and geographic focus, investors can instantly surface investment opportunities that perfectly align with their specific mandates.
* Seamless Partnerships: Users can manage the entire investment lifecycle in one centralized hub. From initial contact and due diligence to secure document sharing, direct messaging, and finalizing term sheets, every crucial milestone is tracked effortlessly.
